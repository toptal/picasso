### Menu

- move focus between items with `ArrowUp`, `ArrowDown`, `Home` and `End`, skipping disabled items
- activate the focused item with `Enter` or `Space`
- open a nested menu with `ArrowRight`, `Enter` or `Space` and focus its first item; `ArrowLeft` closes a drilldown submenu and returns focus to its parent item, or goes back one level in a slide menu
- add `autoFocus` to focus the selected or first enabled item when the menu mounts
